Direct your film
before you shoot it.

Previs Studio is a 3D previs and virtual-directing tool for filmmakers. Build the set, block the actors, shoot your coverage with real lenses, animate characters, cars and cameras, light the scene, steer the camera with your phone — and export the animatic.

Everything previs needs, in one window

Made for directors, DPs, storyboard artists and students: think in shots and lenses, not in 3D software.

📜

Screenplay → Film

Paste a script in any language: every scene is built with its set, cast and timed blocking, lighting and shots — shared sets where a place returns.

🎬

Shots & real lenses

Cameras with focal length, sensor, aspect ratio, focus and frame guides. Build your coverage as a shot list with per-shot cheats.

🧱

400 ready models

Characters, animals, furniture, lamps and chandeliers, buildings and towers, vehicles, military, street kits, nature, props and film gear.

✨

AI Director

Describe a scene in any language — set, blocking, shots, camera moves and lighting are built for you. Works offline from a brief, or with Claude — even through your normal Claude chat, no API key.

🎥

Cinematic camera motion

Dolly, crane, orbit, arc, follow, handheld, drone and more; timing, smoothing, tracking, rigs and shake as layers.

✏️

Draw the move

Sketch the camera route from above — or the route of an actor or a car — and it becomes the animation.

🚶

Actors walk, cars drive

Keyframe characters and vehicles: walk / jog / run picked by speed, feet that do not slide, wheels that roll and steer.

💡

Lighting

Three-point, Rembrandt, low-key, golden hour, moonlight… in one click. Kelvin, stops, practical lamps, time of day, exposure.

📱

Phone as a camera

Scan a QR code and turn your phone — the shot camera follows. Record handheld takes over your own Wi-Fi, no internet.

🎬

Film, scenes & sets

Sequences of scenes, each with its own set, light and cast. Reuse a set in many scenes, then play or export the whole film from one timeline.

📦

Your own models

Import .glb, .gltf, .fbx and .obj: sizes converted to metres, own materials or clay, saved with your projects.

🔊

Sound

Dialogue, music and effects tracks on the scene’s time: in sync across cuts, in playback and in the exported animatic.

🖼️

Reference images

Lay a storyboard panel or a reference frame over the shot camera to match it; place photos as panels in the set.

📋

Storyboard PDF

Every shot as a panel through its camera, with size, angle, lens, move, action and notes — for a scene or the whole film.

🪄

AI prompt & frames

First and last frame of every shot plus an English prompt written from your real scene — for Seedance, Kling, Veo, Runway and more.

🎞️

Animatic export

Every shot through its camera into one MP4, with shot names, lens and timecode burned in. 720p to 4K.

“An interrogation room, two people across a table…”

Write what you see. The Director plans the room with doors and windows, places furniture and actors, frames each shot by size, angle and lens, moves the cameras and lights the set — as one undo step you can refine in plain words.

Draw the camera path

From the top view, draw any line: curves, S-bends, loops. Choose the height, look along the path or keep a subject in frame, set the duration and ease — it becomes smooth camera keys.

Actors that walk, cars that drive

Give a character two keys — or draw its route — and it walks there, turns to face where it goes and stands idle when it arrives. Faster becomes a jog or a run. Vehicles roll their wheels and steer into turns.

Your phone is the camera

Scan the QR code, hold the phone like a camera and move. Record a handheld take while the scene plays; it becomes keyframes you can polish. Lens buttons, a move pad and a lift slider are on the phone. Everything stays on your local network.

Questions

Is it free?Yes. The AI Director uses your own Anthropic (Claude) API key, billed to your account — everything else is free and needs no account.
Does it need internet?No. The desktop app runs fully offline. Phone control uses your local Wi-Fi only. Only Claude needs internet.
Why does Windows / macOS warn me?The installers are not code-signed yet. The warnings are normal for unsigned apps — see the steps above or the manual.
Where are my projects?Saved automatically inside the app; use Save to file for a .previs.json you can share or back up.
Is the source code available?No — Previs Studio is free to use; the source code is private.
